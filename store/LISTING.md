# Chrome Web Store listing — X Tweets Cleaner

**Name:** X Tweets Cleaner
**Category:** Social & Communication (or Productivity)
**Language:** English

**Short description (max 132 chars):**
Clean up your X account: delete your replies, your posts, or both, and undo reposts. Runs in your browser only.

**Detailed description:**
X Tweets Cleaner tidies your X (Twitter) account straight from your profile, with no API keys, logins or servers.

• Choose what to clean: only replies, only posts, or everything
• Optionally undo your reposts too
• Preview mode outlines what would be removed before anything is deleted
• Human-like pacing: a 4-second pause (plus random extra) between actions
• Stop at any time from the popup or the on-page panel
• Works with X in English and Arabic

How to use: open x.com, click the extension, press "Open my Replies tab", choose what to clean, run Preview, then Clean now.

Deleting can't be undone.

---

## Privacy tab (single purpose + permissions)

**Single purpose:** Delete the signed-in user's own posts, replies and reposts on x.com.

**Permission justifications:**
- `storage`: remembers your settings (mode, delay) and shows progress in the popup.
- `tabs`: reads the active tab's address to check you're on x.com and to open your Replies tab.
- Host access to x.com / twitter.com: runs the cleaner on your own profile page.

**Remote code:** No, all code is in the package.

**Data usage:** Collects no user data. Nothing is sent anywhere; everything runs locally in the browser.

## Privacy policy (paste on a page, e.g. falak.tools/privacy or a GitHub page)

X Tweets Cleaner does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data. Settings are saved only in your browser's local extension storage. The extension acts only on x.com pages you open, and only when you press a button.
